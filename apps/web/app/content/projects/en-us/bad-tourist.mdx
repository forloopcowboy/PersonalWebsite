## Summary

Bad Tourist is a multiplayer cooperative tourism arcade game built in Unity with Netcode for GameObjects. Players explore levels together, complete activities, and earn accolades — all under server-authoritative networking.

## Tech

- **Engine**: Unity (URP) with Netcode for GameObjects
- **Language**: C#
- **Architecture**: Server-authoritative with request-validate-broadcast pattern
- **Character system**: Custom HLSL shader for face compositing, ScriptableObject-driven presets and archetypes
- **Activity system**: Modular building blocks (timers, proximity triggers, scoring) composed into activities
