## Character Customization

I wanted a really simple, goofy, cartoonish look to keep the vibes hilarious while also allowing
the player (and the crowd system) to generate a good variety of personalities.

The head customization shader composites facial features from texture atlases onto a UV sphere and lets
you pick translation and scale. Not too fancy, but I fell in love with the silly look and
could not stop myself from making little guys all weekend.

## Summary

Bad Tourist is a multiplayer cooperative tourism arcade game built in Unity with Netcode for GameObjects. Players explore levels together, complete activities, and earn accolades — all under server-authoritative networking.
