## Meet the Ballheads

I wanted a really simple, goofy, cartoonish look to keep the vibes hilarious while still allowing
a good variety of personalities.

The head customization shader composites facial features from texture atlases onto a UV sphere and lets
you pick translation and scale. Not too fancy, but I fell in love with the silly look and
could not stop myself from making little guys all weekend.

Try it out:

<HeadEditor />

Hit **Share** to copy a link to your character. Paste it anywhere and the preview shows
your ballhead's face.

## What's it for?

The Ballhead Character Editor is the character creator for a multiplayer co-op game I'm making about
doing silly things with friends. That's all I'm saying for now.

Characters created in this editor will be importable into the game, so make someone you'd want to
play as.

## Where to find it

- **Demos**: the first demos will be published on [itch.io](https://forloopcowboy.itch.io/).
- **Steam**: the store page will be announced soon.

## Tech

- **Rendering**: three.js with a custom GLSL shader
- **Sharing**: the whole character is packed into a compact string in the URL
- **Link previews**: rendered server-side, so every shared character gets its own face as the image
